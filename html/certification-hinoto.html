🎯 Practical Test Components

Demonstrate B tournament medalist skills through advanced practical testing with elite-level challenges.

🎾 Hinoto Serve/Stack Test

Pass: 38/45 legal serves (84%)
Setup & Requirements:
  • 45 serves total: 25 varied depth, 20 stacked with partner movement
  • Partner moves during stacked serves for dynamic positioning
  • After each serve: return deep + rush to kitchen
  • Tournament-level pressure and timing
Pass Criteria:
  • 38/45 total legal serves (84%)
  • 35/45 kitchen holds before opponent's 3rd shot
  • Advanced stacking execution with partner coordination
  • Consistent serve placement under pressure
💡 Tips:

Master dynamic stacking with partner movement. Focus on consistent placement over power.

🍃 Soft Game Dominance Test

Pass: Win 60% points over 20 minutes
Setup & Execution:
  • Kitchen line vs 4.0 level tester
  • Pure dinks to exhaustion (no drives or volleys)
  • Target feet and sideline areas consistently
  • Sustain 60+ shot rallies when possible
  • Multiple games over 20-minute period
Pass Criteria:
  • Win 60%+ points across 20-minute session
  • Maintain <8% error rate throughout
  • Demonstrate superior soft game control
  • Consistent targeting of weak areas
💡 Tips:

Focus on patience and precision. Let opponent make errors rather than forcing attacks.

⚡ Aggressive Dink/Attack Test

Pass: 15/20 roll dinks + 12/20 attacks
Setup & Execution:
  • Kitchen vs advanced tester
  • 20 total sequences with strategic execution
  • Inside-out roll dink → speed-up or body attack
  • Focus on low, heavy roll dinks with spin
Pass Criteria:
  • 15/20 roll dinks land low/heavy in target (75%)
  • 12/20 attacks force errors or weak replies (60%)
  • Demonstrate proper attack timing and placement
  • Execute with precision and tactical awareness
💡 Tips:

Use roll dinks to set up angles. Attack when opponent is out of position or shows weakness.

🏓 Overhead & Defense Test

Pass: Overhead mastery + pressure defense
A. Overhead Mastery:
  • Setup: Tester lobs high balls with varying difficulty
  • Execution: Alternate defensive lob resets + offensive pummels
  • Target: 15/20 controlled overheads to deep court
  • Focus: No shanks, proper contact point, tactical placement
B. Pressure Defense:
  • Setup: Tester hammers pace with drives and volleys
  • Execution: Block volley/reset to kitchen unattackable
  • Target: 25/30 unattackable returns (83%)
  • Focus: Fault-free NVZ play under pressure
💡 Tips:

Master overhead footwork and timing. Practice defensive resets with soft hands and proper positioning.

🏆 Full Doubles Match

Pass: Win match vs certified 4.0 pair
Format & Setup:
  • Best 2/3 games to 11 points vs certified 4.0 duo
  • Full tournament rules and professional atmosphere
  • Competitive B-level tournament pressure
  • Certified officials if available
Evaluator Checks:
  • 80%+ serves land in legal positions
  • 70%+ third-shot drop success rate
  • Advanced stacking execution with partner
  • Less than 15% unforced errors
  • Excellent partner communication and positioning
  • Tournament-level strategy and adaptation
Pass Criteria:
  • Win match (2-0 or 2-1 victory)
  • Average game score: 11-6 or better
  • Demonstrate consistent elite-level execution
  • Show tactical mastery and mental toughness
💡 Tips:

Trust your training and play within your strengths. Stay calm under tournament pressure.
