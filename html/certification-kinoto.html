🎯 Practical Test Components

Demonstrate national-level competitive skills through comprehensive testing scenarios. All components meet strict national tournament standards.

🎾 Kinoto Serve/Stack Test

Pass: 45/50 legal + pro returns
Setup & Requirements:
  • 50 serves total (25 stacked drop serves + 25 deep spin serves)
  • Pattern returns required: deep → inside-out dink
  • 90% legal serve rate (45/50) minimum
  • 42/50 kitchen holds with pro-level first dink
  • Advanced stacking coordination throughout
Performance Standards:
  • Consistent spin and placement control
  • Perfect stacking timing and execution
  • Pro-level return patterns
  • Advanced kitchen positioning
💡 Tips:

Master PPA stacking rules completely. Focus on precise timing and pro-level return execution.

⚡ Pro Shots Test

Pass: 15/20 BH Flicks + 20/25 Roll Volleys
Part A - Backhand Flicks:
  • Kitchen feeds for 2-hand BH flick speed-ups
  • Target sideline/body placement
  • 15/20 unreturnable winners required
  • Must be disguised from dink setup
Part B - Roll Volleys:
  • Continuous kitchen rolls (forehand/backhand)
  • 5-shot chains required
  • 20/25 complete chains dipping low/heavy
  • Advanced touch and control
💡 Tips:

Practice flick mechanics extensively. Master roll volley touch for consistent low, heavy balls.

🎯 Stacking Execution Test

Pass: 10/15 perfect sequences
Setup & Requirements:
  • Full stacking serves/returns vs 4.5 DUPR tester
  • I-formation and full stack execution
  • Return crosscourt/pinch patterns
  • 15 total sequences attempted
  • Positioning + execution must be perfect
Success Criteria:
  • 10/15 fault-free stacking sequences
  • Perfect timing and coordination
  • Correct positioning throughout
  • Advanced stacking pattern mastery
💡 Tips:

Practice with position tape on court. Master I-formation and full stack variations extensively.

⏱️ Endurance & Pressure Test

Pass: 30 min, <15% UE, 60%+ points won
Setup & Requirements:
  • Continuous play vs 4.0-4.5 DUPR tester
  • 30 minutes sustained match play
  • Track unforced errors throughout
  • Maintain winning percentage
  • National-level consistency required
Performance Standards:
  • Complete 30 minutes without fatigue breakdown
  • Less than 15% unforced error rate
  • Win 60%+ of total points
  • Sustain national-level intensity
💡 Tips:

Build endurance through 45-minute grind matches. Track errors via DUPR app for analysis.
