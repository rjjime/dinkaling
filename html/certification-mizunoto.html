Mizunoto Certification Overview

This certification test focuses on core pickleball basics essential for survival at the 2.0-2.2 DUPR level. You'll demonstrate rule knowledge, consistent serves, basic shot-making, and game awareness.

📝 Written Rules Quiz

Test your knowledge of fundamental pickleball rules. You need 8 out of 10 correct to pass this section.

1. What is the non-volley zone (NVZ or "kitchen")?



2. Can you volley (hit without letting it bounce) while standing in the kitchen?



3. Which of these is NOT required for a legal serve?



4. Who can score points in pickleball?



5. Which of these is a fault that ends your turn?



6. In doubles, where does the server start when the score is even?



7. What happens if you step on the kitchen line while volleying?



8. How do you determine if a ball is "in" or "out"?



9. What is a "let serve"?



10. After the serve and return, how many bounces must each team allow?



🎯 Practical Test Components

Demonstrate your skills through hands-on testing. All practical components must be completed with 80% overall pass rate.

🎾 Serve Test

Pass: 20/30 legal serves (66%)
Requirements:
  • From behind baseline, underhand continental grip
  • Paddle contact below waist
  • 10 deep serves (past NVZ line)
  • 10 short serves (kitchen landing)
  • No foot faults, all diagonal/crosscourt
💡 Tips:

Focus on consistent contact point and smooth follow-through. Practice both power and placement.

🏓 Groundstroke Rally Test

Pass: 10 rallies of 4+ shots
Requirements:
  • Partner feeds: alternate forehand/backhand drives from baseline
  • Keep ball in play, one bounce each side
  • Aim crosscourt for consistency
  • No unforced errors (double-hits, long, wide, net)
💡 Tips:

Stay behind the baseline, use continental grip, focus on placement over power.

🍃 Dink Rally Test

Pass: 30+ shot rally without faults
Requirements:
  • Both players at kitchen line
  • Soft dinks only (no volleys)
  • Ball must land in opponent's kitchen, below net height
  • Sustain rally for 5 minutes
💡 Tips:

Use soft continental grip, minimal swing, focus on placement and patience.

🎯 Game Simulation

Pass: Competitive score vs 2.5 tester
Requirements:
  • Play one game to 11, win by 2
  • vs. certified 2.5 level tester
  • Demonstrate proper positioning (move to kitchen after return)
  • Show basic strategy (attempt third-shot drop)
  • No rules disputes
💡 Tips:

Focus on consistency and following basic strategy. A close loss (11-9) is acceptable.

🏋️ Practice Recommendations

Recommended Drills:
  • Shadow Swings: Practice form without ball
  • Wall Dinks: Develop touch and control
  • Serve Practice: 50 serves daily focusing on consistency
  • Rally Practice: Crosscourt groundstroke rallies
  • Kitchen Positioning: Move to kitchen line after return
