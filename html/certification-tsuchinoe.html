🎯 Practical Test Components

Demonstrate C-level tournament regular skills through advanced practical testing with pressure scenarios.

🎾 Tsuchinoe Serve/Return Test

Pass: 35/40 serves + 30/40 transitions
Setup & Requirements:
  • 40 serves: 20 deep/medium, 10 short drops, 10 stacked
  • After each serve: return deep + rush to kitchen
  • Test kitchen positioning and transition timing
  • Simulate tournament serving pressure
Pass Criteria:
  • 35/40 total legal serves (88%)
  • 30/40 kitchen holds before opponent's 3rd shot
  • Proper serve placement and spin control
  • Quick, efficient court movement
💡 Tips:

Focus on serve placement over power. Practice quick transitions from serve to kitchen positioning.

🏓 Rally Control Test

Pass: Extended rallies + roll volleys
A. Extended Rally Test:
  • Setup: Baseline crosscourt, FH/BH alternation
  • Target: 15 rallies of 10+ shots each (150+ total shots)
  • Focus: Depth variation and consistency
  • Pass: 15/15 rallies with <15% error rate
B. Roll Volley Test:
  • Setup: Kitchen line, partner feeds bounces
  • Execution: Roll volley back low/heavy with topspin
  • Target: Feet area, dip into opponent's kitchen
  • Pass: 15/20 successful roll volleys (75%)
💡 Tips:

Master the low-to-high swing path for roll volleys. Focus on dip and placement over pace.

🍃 Dink/Attack Patterns Test

Pass: 16/20 dinks + 12/20 attacks
Setup & Execution:
  • Kitchen crosscourt vs 3.75 level tester
  • 20 total exchanges with strategic patterns
  • 10 inside-out dinks → speed-up attacks
  • 10 straight dinks → body attacks
Pass Criteria:
  • 16/20 dinks land in target areas (80%)
  • 12/20 attacks force weak returns or errors
  • Demonstrate proper pattern recognition
  • Execute attacks with appropriate timing
💡 Tips:

Read opponent positioning carefully. Use inside-out dinks to set up angles, straight dinks for body targets.

🎯 Third-Shot & Defense Test

Pass: Mixed shots + defensive resets
A. Third-Shot Mix (Advanced):
  • Setup: Alternate soft drops and aggressive drives
  • Target: 20/25 land correctly (80%)
  • Focus: Disguise and tactical variety
  • Challenge: Maintain consistency under pressure
B. Defensive Resets:
  • Setup: Tester hammers hard drives
  • Execution: Block/reset to kitchen softly
  • Target: 20/25 return unattackable to kitchen
  • Focus: Defensive positioning and soft hands
💡 Tips:

Practice defensive reset footwork. Use the "soft hands, absorb pace" technique for resets.

🏆 Mini-Match Simulation

Pass: Win 2/3 games decisively
Format & Setup:
  • 2-3 games to 11 points vs certified 3.5 tester
  • Full tournament rules and pressure
  • Competitive C-level atmosphere
  • Rotating partners if available
Evaluator Checks:
  • 75%+ serves land in legal positions
  • 65%+ third-shot drop success rate
  • Partner synchronization and communication
  • Less than 20% unforced errors
  • Tournament-level strategy execution
Pass Criteria:
  • Win both games or 2/3 decisively (11-6+ scores)
  • Demonstrate consistent technique under pressure
  • Show tactical awareness and adaptation
💡 Tips:

Focus on execution over results. Let your trained skills flow naturally in game situations.
