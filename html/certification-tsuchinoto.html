📝 Tsuchinoto Rules Quiz

Demonstrate advanced rules knowledge including stacking, volleys, and tournament-specific regulations. Perfect 10/10 required.

1. What is "stacking" in doubles, and is it legal?



2. Can you volley a ball that has bounced in the NVZ if you're outside it?



3. Name 2 differences between PPA and USA Pickleball serving rules.



4. What is a "screen serve" fault?



5. During a rally, if your paddle hits the net post but ball clears, is it good?



6. Explain "paddle present" rule for NVZ faults.



7. In doubles, can the receiver cross the centerline during return?



8. What happens if both teams fault simultaneously?



9. Is a drop serve (arcing into NVZ) legal in standard play?



10. Define "technical fault" vs "player fault."



🎯 Practical Test Components

Demonstrate local tournament-qualifier skills through advanced practical testing with new techniques.

🎾 Tsuchinoto Serve Test

Pass: 35/40 legal serves (88%)
Setup & Requirements:
  • 40 serves total, including 10 stacking simulations
  • 20 deep/medium serves (targets as previous levels)
  • 10 short drop serves (just past NVZ)
  • 10 stacked serves (partner positioned same side)
Stacking Component:
  • Partner positioned same side for serves
  • Serve from both left and right positions
  • Maintain proper service box positioning
  • 8/10 stacked serves must be successful
Pass Criteria:
  • 35/40 total legal serves (88%)
  • 8/10 stacking serves successful
  • No foot faults or NVZ violations
💡 Tips:

Practice stacking formations extensively. Master serving from both sides of the court.

🏓 Groundstroke/Volley Intro Test

Pass: Drive rallies + soft volleys
A. Drive Rally Test:
  • Setup: Baseline to baseline, one bounce per side
  • Execution: Topspin drives with depth variation
  • Target: Deep/midcourt targets across court
  • Pass: 20/25 land in target zones (80%)
B. Soft Volley Introduction:
  • Setup: Kitchen line, partner feeds bounces
  • Execution: Punch volleys below net height
  • Focus: Clean contact, no NVZ faults
  • Pass: 10/15 clean, fault-free kitchen volleys
💡 Tips:

Introduce volleys gradually. Focus on soft hands and control rather than power.

🍃 Dink Patterns Test

Pass: 16/20 dinks + 7/10 attacks
Setup & Execution:
  • Kitchen crosscourt dinks vs 3.5 level tester
  • 20 forehand inside-out dinks (angle wide to opponent's backhand)
  • Follow 10 dinks with speed-up attacks to middle
  • Focus on pattern recognition and execution
Pass Criteria:
  • 16/20 inside-out dinks land in target area
  • 7/10 speed-ups result in winners or unattackable balls
  • Demonstrate proper dink-angle-attack pattern
  • Maintain kitchen positioning throughout
💡 Tips:

Read opponent positioning. Use inside-out dinks to open up attack opportunities.

🎯 Third-Shot & Overhead Test

Pass: Both drops and overheads
A. Third-Shot Drops (Advanced):
  • Setup: Baseline after partner's return
  • Execution: Arcing drops to kitchen with disguise
  • Target: 20/25 soft kitchen landings (80%)
  • Focus: Disguise with occasional drive fakes
B. Overhead Introduction:
  • Setup: Partner lobs high balls for practice
  • Execution: Side stance, contact high point
  • Options: Alternate lob resets vs pummels (drives)
  • Pass: 12/15 controlled overheads (no shanks/long)
💡 Tips:

Practice overhead timing and footwork. Master the side stance approach.

🏆 Mini-Tournament Simulation

Pass: Win 2/3 games to 11
Format & Setup:
  • 3 doubles games to 11 points vs 2.6-2.8 level testers
  • Full tournament rules and scoring
  • Rotating partners if available
  • Competitive tournament atmosphere
Evaluator Checks:
  • Stacking execution when appropriate
  • Proper volley use and timing
  • Pattern play: dink-angle-attack sequences
  • Less than 20% unforced errors
  • Positive partner feedback and communication
Pass Criteria:
  • Win 2 out of 3 games (e.g., 11-7 scores)
  • Demonstrate tournament-level strategy
  • Consistent execution of new techniques
💡 Tips:

Focus on applying all learned techniques in game situations. Stay patient and strategic.
