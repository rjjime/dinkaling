🥉 Mizunoto/Mizunoe/Kanoto Certification Test

Ready to earn your first Dinkaling certification? This 2.0–3.0 DUPR level test focuses on basic rules knowledge, consistent serves, and fault-free play.

Test Overview

Pass Requirements: 80% overall pass rate across all components

Duration: Approximately 45-60 minutes

Retest Policy: Allowed after 2 weeks of practice

📝 Written Rules Quiz

Pass: 8/10 correct

1. What is the non-volley zone?

Show Answer

The non-volley zone (NVZ or kitchen) is the 7-foot area on both sides of the net where volleys are prohibited.

2. Can you volley in the kitchen? Why?

Show Answer

No, volleying in the kitchen is a fault; the ball must bounce first after crossing the net.

3. Legal serve requirements?

Show Answer

Underhand motion, paddle contact below waist/navel, arm straightens fully at contact, diagonal crosscourt, one attempt per serve.

4. Scoring basics?

Show Answer

Only the serving team can score points; the receiving team must win serve back first.

5. What faults end your point?

Show Answer

Foot faults, NVZ violations, ball out of bounds/net faults, double-bounces, illegal serves.

6. Doubles rotation rules?

Show Answer

Server starts right/even score, switches sides after losing serve; partner serves from left after winning point.

7. Kitchen line violations?

Show Answer

Stepping into/on NVZ line during volley or momentum carrying you in after volley.

8. Ball in/out calls?

Show Answer

Whole ball over line = in; any part over = out (line calls belong to opponents).

9. Let serve rules?

Show Answer

Let serve (clips net but lands in correct service box) is replayed; no point awarded.

10. One-bounce rule after serve/return?

Show Answer

After serve and return, each side gets one bounce before volleying (two-bounce rule).

🎾 Serve Test

Pass: 20/30 legal serves (66%)
Requirements:
  • From behind baseline, underhand continental grip
  • Paddle contact below waist
  • 10 deep serves (past NVZ line)
  • 10 short serves (kitchen landing)
  • No foot faults, all diagonal/crosscourt
💡 Tips:

Focus on consistent contact point and smooth follow-through. Practice both power and placement.

🏓 Groundstroke Rally Test

Pass: 10 rallies of 4+ shots
Requirements:
  • Partner feeds: alternate forehand/backhand drives from baseline
  • Keep ball in play, one bounce each side
  • Aim crosscourt for consistency
  • No unforced errors (double-hits, long, wide, net)
💡 Tips:

Stay behind the baseline, use continental grip, focus on placement over power.

🍃 Dink Rally Test

Pass: 30+ shot rally without faults
Requirements:
  • Both players at kitchen line
  • Soft dinks only (no volleys)
  • Ball must land in opponent's kitchen, below net height
  • Sustain rally for 5 minutes
💡 Tips:

Use soft continental grip, minimal swing, focus on placement and patience.

🎯 Game Simulation

Pass: Competitive score vs 2.5 tester
Requirements:
  • Play one game to 11, win by 2
  • vs. certified 2.5 level tester
  • Demonstrate proper positioning (move to kitchen after return)

🏋️ Practice Recommendations

Recommended Drills:
  • Shadow Swings: Practice form without ball
  • Wall Dinks: Develop touch and control
  • Serve Practice: 50 serves daily focusing on consistency
  • Rally Practice: Crosscourt groundstroke rallies
  • Kitchen Positioning: Move to kitchen line after return
